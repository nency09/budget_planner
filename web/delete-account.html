Delete your FinGenie account

Use this page to request deletion of your FinGenie account and associated app data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vurlexbusiness@gmail.com from the email address used for your FinGenie account.
  2. Use the subject line FinGenie Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your account email address and confirm that you want your account deleted.

Data deleted

After verifying the request, we delete the Firebase account associated with your email address and any account data stored by FinGenie on our Firebase services.

Data not controlled by FinGenie

Local data stored only on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data from your device settings. Backups stored in your personal Google Drive are controlled by your Google account and can be deleted from Google Drive by you.

Retention

Account deletion requests are usually processed within 7 days. We may retain limited records if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or purchase/account dispute handling.

Developer

FinGenie is published by Vurlex. For privacy details, see the FinGenie Privacy Policy.
